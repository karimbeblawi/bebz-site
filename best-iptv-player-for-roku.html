Best IPTV player for Roku: how to choose

People search for the best IPTV player for Roku when they already have (or will get) an IPTV playlist and want it on a Roku TV. A player is not a channel pack. The app plays your Xtream Codes or M3U list. It should not sell you live TV.

What the best IPTV player for Roku must do

  • Play IPTV on Roku from Xtream Codes and M3U.
  • Show live TV, movies, and series from that playlist.
  • Let you try the IPTV Roku player before paying.
  • Say clearly that content is yours, not the app’s.
  • Stay in the Roku Channel Store so you can install it on the TV.
